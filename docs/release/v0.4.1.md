# LocalReel 0.4.1 release notes

A small UI follow-up to 0.4.0, driven by how the feed actually looks on a phone.

## Changes

- **The language switch no longer competes with the video.** 中文 / EN were two
  large pills in the reels top bar; they are now one small globe button that
  shows the current language and toggles on tap. The reels back button is
  smaller too. The UI still follows the browser language and remembers the
  choice.
- **Landscape video gets a "全屏观看" (~Full screen) pill**, placed directly
  under the picture like Douyin's. Tapping it fullscreens the player and asks
  the OS to lock landscape on Android; iOS falls back to the native video
  player. The video stays letterboxed (never cropped) on a blurred thumbnail
  backdrop.
- **Temporary speed is now right-side only.** Long-press the right third of the
  video to play at 2×, release to restore 1×. The left side no longer triggers
  it, leaving that area for tap, double-tap and swipe.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.4.0; the database is unchanged. `data/` and `localreel.toml` are
kept.
